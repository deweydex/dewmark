# Synthesis notes: where the designs disagree, and how each disagreement was settled

*Design round, 2026-09-28. Written alongside `planning/PROPOSAL.md`. The five designs in this folder (`architecture.md`, `student-flow.md`, `question-types.md`, `assistant.md`, and the judged `format.md`) and the two mockups were written in parallel, most of them before Josh answered the twenty questions in `planning/DECISIONS_2026-09-27.md`. This file lists every contradiction found, what settles it, and where it lands in the proposal. "Decision n" means Josh's decision n; "D n" means a decision the proposal puts to Josh.*

Three kinds of settlement:

- **Josh** — one of the twenty decisions settles it; the design gives way.
- **Evidence** — a probe, a later and better-informed design, or a constraint in the brief settles it.
- **Open** — neither does; it goes to Josh in `PROPOSAL.md` §2.
